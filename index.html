What's on your tab

Everything for a mindful day, in one glance

No extra clicks, no separate apps — it's the first thing you see.

🕌

Prayer Times

Calculated for your exact location, with 7 methods to choose from — including Indonesia's official Kemenag RI.

🧭

Qibla Direction

A precise bearing to the Ka'bah, computed the moment your location is known — no separate compass app.

🌙

Upcoming Islamic Dates

A running countdown to Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, and the rest of the Hijri calendar's milestones.

📿

Daily Reminders

One gentle nudge a day for dhikr and Qur'an reading, at a time you choose — never naggy, never five-times-a-day loud.

📖

Qur'an & Hadith

A rotating quote to sit with, in Indonesian, English, or Arabic — sourced and cited every time.

📝

Todo & Weather

A quiet to-do list with backup/restore, plus the day's weather — the practical half of your morning glance.

Built to stay quiet about you

No backend, no accounts, no analytics. Your data stays on your device — full stop.

  • To-dos, preferences, and reminders live only in your browser's local storage.
  • Location is sent straight to the prayer-time & weather APIs that need it — never to a server of ours, because none exists.
  • No ads, no trackers, no sign-up.
